Common questions

Can I play Burmese chess in Burmaland?

Yes. Burmese chess (ဗမာစစ်တုရင်) is one of the features inside Burmaland, alongside local content and the Burma–Myanmar dictionary.

Which platforms does Burmaland support?

Burmaland is available on Android through Google Play, on iPhone and iPad through the App Store, and on desktop through a macOS .dmg and a Windows .exe installer.

Where do I download the desktop app?

The macOS .dmg and Windows .exe installers are hosted in the official Burmaland GitHub repository, linked from the Desktop section of this page.
