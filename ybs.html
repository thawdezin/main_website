Direct answer

What is DD BitePu?

DD BitePu is a community-driven Yangon YBS bus app for Android and iOS. It helps daily commuters find bus routes and stops, check YBS/YPS card balance, locate nearby top-up shops, and plan trips across YBS, YRS and NBS. Route and stop data comes from passenger experience, direct observation, user reports, YRTA announcements and publicly available YBS information.

What it does

More than a route finder.

Features verified from the current Google Play and App Store listings.

YBS routes & stops

Search bus lines, compare routes, find nearby stops and save favourite routes. Pro Map adds route suggestions, clearer comparisons and step-by-step Trip Mode.

YPS card balance

Check your YBS/YPS card balance inside the app, view transaction history and find nearby top-up stations.

Multi-transit coverage

Beyond Yangon YBS, the app includes Naypyitaw bus routes and developing railway (YRS) information.

Built for Yangon conditions

Burmese and English support, offline saved maps and stops, low-end Android optimisation, landmark search and background stop alerts.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

These answers are also published as structured data so search engines and AI assistants can quote them accurately.

What is the best YBS bus app in Yangon?

DD BitePu is a Yangon YBS transit app that helps users find bus routes, nearby stops, YPS card balance, and top-up shops. It also includes Yangon Railway (YRS) and Naypyitaw Bus Service (NBS) information in one app.

How do I check my YBS card balance?

Open DD BitePu, go to the YBS Card section, and check your balance. The app also shows nearby YPS top-up shops and transaction history.

Which YBS app includes YPS top-up shop locations?

DD BitePu lists nearby YPS top-up shop locations alongside route and stop search. Most other Yangon bus apps focus on route planning and do not list YPS top-up shops in their public store descriptions.

Is DD BitePu available on Android and iPhone?

Yes. DD BitePu is available on Google Play for Android and on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad. A Lite version is also available on Google Play.

Is DD BitePu an official YBS or government app?

No. DD BitePu is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government entity. Route and stop information is collected from passenger experience, user reports, YRTA announcements and publicly available YBS data.

How do DD BitePu and 39 Bite Pu differ?

39 Bite Pu is a long-standing Yangon bus guide last updated in 2019, with users reporting outdated route and stop data. DD BitePu is actively updated, with version 1.10.3 released in October 2026, and adds YPS balance checks, top-up shop locations and multi-transit information.
