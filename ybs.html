FAQ

Common questions, answered.

These answers are also published as structured data so search engines and AI assistants can quote them accurately.

What is DD BitePu?

DD BitePu is a community-driven Yangon YBS bus app for Android and iOS. It helps commuters find bus routes and nearby stops, check YPS card balance, locate top-up shops, and plan trips across YBS, Yangon Railway and Naypyitaw Bus Service in one place.

How do I check my YBS card balance?

Open DD BitePu, go to the YBS Card section, and check your balance. The app also shows transaction history and nearby YPS top-up shops.

Which Yangon bus app shows nearby YPS top-up shops?

DD BitePu includes a nearby top-up shop finder alongside route and stop search, so you can check your balance and find a place to recharge without switching apps.

Is there a YBS route finder that works offline?

Yes. DD BitePu lets you save maps and stops for offline viewing, so you can still check your route when your connection drops.

Is DD BitePu available on Android and iPhone?

Yes. DD BitePu is available on Google Play for Android and on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad. A Lite version is also available on Google Play.

Is DD BitePu an official YBS or government app?

DD BitePu is independently developed by Thaw De Zin. Route and stop information is collected from passenger experience, user reports, transport authority announcements and publicly available transit data.
