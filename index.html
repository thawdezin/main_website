My story

From writing code to creating impact.

My work has always started with curiosity: can this be simpler, kinder, more useful?

I'm a graduate of the University of Technology (Yatanarpon Cyber City) — Bachelor of Engineering in Information Science and Technology, 4th batch, February 2020. The graduation was prior to the 2021 Myanmar coup d'état: I earned my degree in the democratic period, before the later disruption of the country's education system. That formal engineering foundation still shapes how I build: carefully, systematically and with care for the person using the result.

Since my first engineering internship in 2018, I've grown through iOS and cross-platform roles into senior mobile development—while continuing to ship independent products of my own. I stay politically neutral and keep my focus on building useful products, not taking sides.

I was already working in IT before the AI era. I learned systems the hard way — building Linux From Scratch (LFS), exploring FreeBSD and Debian, and fixing Groovy Gradle and Kotlin Gradle errors from logs and documentation long before an assistant could suggest a fix. openSUSE Linux is still my favourite distribution.

I care about the full journey: understanding the person, shaping the experience, engineering for reliability and learning from what happens after launch.

Outside product work: Flutter and Python, Cardano, learning Thai, and a steady supply of coffee.
